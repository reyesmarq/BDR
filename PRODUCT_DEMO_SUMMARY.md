# Product Demo Summary

BDR CRM solves a specific, narrow problem: an individual B2B sales development rep juggling a personal pipeline of leads in spreadsheets, sticky notes, and memory, with no single place to see what needs attention today. Full enterprise CRMs like Salesforce or HubSpot solve this too, but they're built for sales *organizations* — admin consoles, team permissions, pipeline reporting a single rep doesn't need just to keep track of their own accounts and next steps. BDR CRM is the smallest version of that problem solved well: log in, see your accounts and what's due soonest, open one to get full context, and move on to the next call.

The intended user is exactly that — one BDR, managing their own pipeline, not a sales manager overseeing a team. Every account, contact, task, and resource in the system is scoped to the signed-in rep alone; there's no concept of a shared team pipeline or manager view, by design. That scoping is enforced server-side on every request, not just hidden in the UI, so a rep genuinely cannot see or modify another rep's data even by guessing a URL.

The most important flow starts at the dashboard, which a rep lands on after signing in: their accounts (searchable and filterable by status) alongside their follow-up tasks sorted soonest-first. Clicking into an account surfaces everything needed before a call — key contacts, the account's open follow-ups, and past notes — and from there the rep can create a new follow-up task, edit the account's status as the deal progresses, or mark a task complete once it's done. Separately, a Resources page lets the rep save and reuse the email templates and call scripts they actually rely on day to day, instead of rewriting the same cold email for the tenth time. Together, these flows replace the spreadsheet-and-memory approach with one page a rep can open each morning and trust.
